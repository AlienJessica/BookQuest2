# BookQuest

Free, interactive study books for school children: story chapters, games, practice tests and virtual science labs. Everything runs in one web page, works on phones, and saves progress in the browser.

## What is inside

**Class 4** (West Bengal Board of Primary Education books)
- **পাতাবাহার** (Bengali): all 9 units, 27 stories, poems and songs.
- **Butterfly** (English): the revision lesson and lessons 1 to 10.
- Each lesson has:
  - a short summary;
  - author notes and word meanings;
  - games: word matching, putting events in order, "who said it?" and picture puzzles;
  - practice: multiple-choice questions, fill in the blanks and model answers;
  - grammar practice;
  - lesson tests and whole-book tests.

**Class 10** (NCERT, all subjects)
- Story-style chapters with Tara, Kabir and Nanu the robot.
- Pictures, revision notes and chapter tests.
- Seven virtual science labs: Chem Lab, Circuit Lab, Magnet Lab, Light Lab, Genetics Garden, Ecosystem Lab and Bio Bench.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. There is no build step and no server needed.

## About the content

All study material here (summaries, questions, answers, games and illustrations) is written fresh as a study aid. It is not copied from the textbooks. Students should read the full lessons in their own textbooks. The textbooks themselves belong to their publishers. The West Bengal books carry a notice that notes, questions and answers for them should not be published without written approval from the Director of School Education, West Bengal.
